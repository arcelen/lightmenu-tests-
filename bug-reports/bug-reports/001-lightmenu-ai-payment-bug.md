# Bug report 001 - AI incorrect payment Breakdown

**App** lightmenu.app
**Date** 2026-07-23
**Severity** High
**Status** Open

## Summary
The AI agent reports unpaid Bills which is impossible by design - a table cannot be closed without payment 

## steps to reproduce
1. Log into lightmenu.app
2. Open the AI assistant 
3. ask "how many bills do we have since last month" 

## Actual result  
Ai agent reports  8  unpaid bills out of 20 total.

## Impact 
Restaurant owners see incorrect financial data. This could cause confusion in accounting and loss of trust in the AI feature.
